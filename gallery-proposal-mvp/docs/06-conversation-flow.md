# Conversation Flow Examples

## Example 1: Simple Addition

```
[Screen shows: Artwork grid with 8 pieces]

AI: "Hi! Let's create a proposal for Jane Chen. She loves Postwar
    Abstraction and has a budget of $250K-$1M.

    I'm showing 8 works that might interest her. Click any artwork
    to add it, or tell me which ones you like."

User: [clicks Frankenthaler card]

AI: "Added Helen Frankenthaler 'Mountains and Sea' ($1.85M).
    That's above Jane's typical budget range of $250K-$1M.
    Should we add complementary pieces or look at other options?"

User: "Add the Mitchell"

AI: "Added Joan Mitchell 'Ladybug' ($1.2M).

    Current selection:
    • Frankenthaler - $1.85M
    • Mitchell - $1.2M
    Total: $3.05M

    These are both powerful works by pioneering women Color Field
    artists. Want to see the preview?"

[Screen: Frankenthaler and Mitchell cards show checkmarks]

User: "Yes"

AI: "Switching to preview mode..."

[Screen transitions to proposal preview showing both artworks]

AI: "Here's your proposal with 2 artworks totaling $3.05M.
    Ready to export, or would you like to make changes?"

User: "Export it"

AI: "Generating PDF for Jane Chen...

    ✓ Cover page
    ✓ Introduction
    ✓ Summary snapshot
    ✓ Frankenthaler artwork page
    ✓ Mitchell artwork page
    ✓ Terms and contact

    Done! Downloaded 'ChenProposal_2024-11-29.pdf'

    Your 8-page proposal is ready to send. Need anything else?"
```

## Example 2: Natural Language Selection

```
User: "Show me works by women artists"

AI: "Great focus for Jane's collection! We have 3 works by women artists:

    • Helen Frankenthaler - 'Mountains and Sea' ($1.85M)
    • Joan Mitchell - 'Ladybug' ($1.2M)
    • Agnes Martin - 'The Tree' ($1.1M)

    All are museum-quality and align with Jane's interest in Postwar
    Abstraction. Want me to add any of these?"

User: "Add all three"

AI: "Added all 3 women artists to your proposal:

    • Frankenthaler - $1.85M
    • Mitchell - $1.2M
    • Martin - $1.1M
    Total: $4.15M

    This creates a powerful narrative around pioneering women of
    Abstract Expressionism and Minimalism."
```

## Example 3: Budget-Conscious Selection

```
User: "What can we get under $1M?"

AI: "Within Jane's stated budget of $250K-$1M, we have 4 options:

    • Anselm Kiefer - 'Ash Flower' ($420K) - Primary, monumental
    • Robert Motherwell - 'Elegy...' ($750K) - Secondary, iconic series
    • Ellsworth Kelly - 'Red Blue Green' ($890K) - Secondary, triptych
    • Cy Twombly - 'Untitled (Bacchus)' ($980K) - Secondary, late work

    The Kiefer is the only primary market piece - direct from gallery.
    Want to explore any of these?"
```
